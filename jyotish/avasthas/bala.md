# AI Documentation: bala.py (Bala Avastha)

## Logic constraints for AI
* **Base Engine:** Purely mathematical/degree-based. Does NOT use friendships, dignities, or aspects.
* **Degree Logic:** The 30-degree sign is divided into five 6-degree segments.
* **Odd vs Even Parity:** 
  - Odd Signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius): Forward order (Bala, Kumara, Yuva, Vriddha, Mrita).
  - Even Signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces): Reverse order (Mrita, Vriddha, Yuva, Kumara, Bala).
* **Return Value:** Returns a dict with "state" and "energy" (multiplier: 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, 0.125, 0.00).
