1. THE 9 GRAHAS (PLANETARY FORCES) & KARAKAS (SIGNIFICATORS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SURYA (Sun): Atmakaraka (Soul), Ego, Father, Authority, Vitality, Dharma, Royal power.
- CHANDRA (Moon): Manas (Mind/Emotions), Mother, Memory, Public reception, Nurturing.
- MANGALA (Mars): Bhratru (Siblings/Courage), Physical strength, Action, Logic, Land/Property, Conflict.
- BUDHA (Mercury): Buddhi (Intellect/Speech), Trade, Communication, Analytical skill, Learning.
- GURU (Jupiter): Jnana (Wisdom/Guru), Children, Wealth, Expansion, Higher learning, Virtue.
- SHUKRA (Venus): Kalatra (Spouse/Relationships), Luxury, Arts, Comforts, Devotion, Semen/Vital fluids.
- SHANI (Saturn): Ayush (Longevity/Sorrow), Karma, Discipline, Hard work, Delay, Service, Restraint.
- RAHU (North Node): Maya (Illusion/Desire), Obsession, Foreign influences, Innovation, Material expansion.
- KETU (South Node): Moksha (Liberation/Detachment), Intuition, Past-life karma, Asceticism, Sudden insight.

*RULE:* Do NOT use Western outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto). Rashi ownership belongs strictly to the 7 visible Grahas + Rahu/Ketu shadow nodes.

3. GRAHA DRISHTI (PLANETARY ASPECTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*GENERAL ASPECT:* All 9 Grahas aspect the 7th house (180°) counting inclusively from their current placement.
*SPECIAL ASPECTS (VISHEESHA DRISHTI):*
- MANGALA (Mars): Aspects the 4th, 7th, and 8th houses from its position.
- GURU (Jupiter): Aspects the 5th, 7th, and 9th houses from its position (casts divine grace/protection).
- SHANI (Saturn): Aspects the 3rd, 7th, and 10th houses from its position (casts delay/discipline/responsibility).
- RAHU & KETU: Aspect the 5th, 7th, and 9th houses from their positions.

*COUNTING RULE:* Always count the house containing the planet as House 1. (e.g. Jupiter in House 1 aspects House 5, House 7, and House 9).

4. D1 (RASI) VS. D9 (NAVAMSA) DIVISIONAL CHARTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- D1 RASI CHART (Material World & Physical Manifestation):
  * Represents tangible circumstances, physical body, external career, wealth, and day-to-day existence.
  * Shows the birth seed of karma.
- D9 NAVAMSA CHART (Soul Purpose, Internal Strength, & Marriage):
  * Represents the fruit of the tree. Discloses whether a planet in D1 has underlying strength or weakness.
  * Pushkara Navamsa & Vargottama: If a Graha occupies the SAME Rashi sign in both D1 and D9, it is 'Vargottama' and gains immense strength.
  * A planet debilitated in D1 but exalted in D9 undergoes Neecha Bhanga (cancellation of debilitation) and matures into powerful positive karma.
  * D9 specifically governs marital life, spouse's nature, and spiritual alignment (Dharma).

5. VIMSHOTTARI DASHA TIMELINE SYSTEM (TIMING OF KARMA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 120-Year Cycle: Ketu (7y), Shukra (20y), Surya (6y), Chandra (10y), Mangala (7y), Rahu (18y), Guru (16y), Shani (19y), Budha (17y).
- MAHADASHA (Major Period): Sets the overarching climate, theme, and main actors of life for several years.
- ANTARDASHA (Sub-Period): Focuses the Mahadasha into specific events, specific months, and immediate results.
- EVENT TRIGGER RULES:
  * An active Dasha lord triggers the houses it OWNS, the house it OCCUPIES, and the house it ASPECTS in D1 & D9.
  * Benefic Dasha lords (Jupiter, Venus, unafflicted Mercury, bright Moon) in strong dignity produce expansion, prosperity, and peace.
  * Malefic Dasha lords (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Sun) bring tests, transformation, hard work, and structural realignment according to house rulership.
  * Kendras (Houses 1, 4, 7, 10) and Trikonas (Houses 1, 5, 9) bring auspicious growth. Dusthanas (Houses 6, 8, 12) bring challenges, disease, transformation, or foreign travel.
