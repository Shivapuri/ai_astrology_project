# Contextual & Macro Setup Yogas Engine

## Overview & Foundational Purpose
Contextual Setup Yogas evaluate the macrocosmic architecture of the native's chart before individual grahas and house rulers are analyzed in isolation. They reveal the **overall distribution pattern, psychological scope, and structural support** of the native's life canvas.

This subsystem implements ~25 classical Parāśarī yogas across 7 functional groups:
1. **Sāṅkhya Yogas (Pattern of Distribution - 7 Yogas)**: Tallying the number of unique zodiac signs occupied by the classical 7 grahas.
2. **General Scope Yogas (Sun-Moon Quadrant Geometry - 3 Yogas)**: Evaluating the angular distance between the Moon (Manas) and the Sun (Soul/Ahaṁkāra).
3. **Mahābhāgya Yoga (Supreme Fortune Synergies - 2 Yogas)**: Synergizing Sect (Day/Night) with gender and sign polarities (Odd/Even).
4. **Śubha & Aśubha Solitary House Yogas (4 Yogas)**: Solitary occupants or flanking of the 1st house (Tanu Bhāva).
5. **3-Tier Kemadruma Yoga & Bhaṅga (Lunar Isolation & Rescue - 2 Yogas)**: Diagnosing subjective mental isolation versus self-reliant detachment.
6. **Solar Flanking Yogas (Veśi, Vośi, Ubhayācarī - 6 Yogas)**: Planetary energy flanking the Sun (excluding Moon, Rahu, Ketu).
7. **Dual-Lagna Raja Yoga (Ubhayalagna Synchrony - 1 Yoga)**: Simultaneous Kendra-Koṇa combinations from both Janma Lagna and Candra Lagna.

---

## 1. Sāṅkhya Yogas (Signs Occupied by Classical 7 Grahas)
- **Contestants:** Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn.
- **Rules:**
  - **1 Sign: Gola Yoga** — Extreme concentration, narrow one-pointed focus.
  - **2 Signs: Yuga Yoga** — Dualistic tension, polarization, reliance on partners.
  - **3 Signs: Śūla Yoga** — Trident resolve, sharp ambition, austere fortitude.
  - **4 Signs: Kedāra Yoga** — Agricultural patience, grounded endurance, steady builder.
  - **5 Signs: Pāśa Yoga** — Tied to family, societal obligations, administrative networking.
  - **6 Signs: Dāma Yoga** — Generous benefactor, broad-minded, widely accomplished.
  - **7 Signs: Vīṇā Yoga** — Harmonious versatility, musical/aesthetic grace, balanced life.

---

## 2. General Scope Yogas (Sun-Moon Quadrants)
Calculated as the whole-sign distance from Sun to Moon:
- **Kendra Scope (Houses 1, 4, 7, 10 from Sun):** High visibility, prominent self-expression.
- **Panaphara Scope (Houses 2, 5, 8, 11 from Sun):** Moderate resources, steady middle-phase growth.
- **Apoklima Scope (Houses 3, 6, 9, 12 from Sun):** Subdued public stance, introspective and contemplative depth.

---

## 3. Mahābhāgya Yoga
- **Male Configuration:** Day birth + Odd Lagna + Odd Sun + Odd Moon.
- **Female Configuration:** Night birth + Even Lagna + Even Sun + Even Moon.

---

## 4. Śubha & Aśubha 1st House Yogas
- **Aśubha Tanu (Solitary Mars/Saturn in 1st):** High physical grit at the expense of gentleness.
- **Śubha Tanu (Solitary Benefic in 1st):** Natural grace, charisma, and social protection.
- **Śubhakartari / Pāpakartari of 1st House:** Benefics or malefics flanking 2nd and 12th houses from Lagna.

---

## 5. 3-Tier Kemadruma Yoga & Cancellation
- **Tier 1 (Trigger):** No planets (excluding Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in 2nd or 12th from Moon.
- **Tier 2 (Core Check):** Moon not occupying a Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Lagna.
- **Tier 3 (Complete Isolation):** No classical planets in Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) from Moon.
- **Cancellation (*Kemadruma Bhaṅga*):** If Tier 1 is present, but Moon is in Kendra from Lagna or classical planets occupy Kendras from Moon.

---

## 6. Solar Flanking Yogas (Veśi, Vośi, Ubhayācarī)
Inspecting 2nd and 12th houses from the Sun (excluding Moon, Rahu, Ketu):
- **Veśi Yoga:** Planet in 2nd from Sun (*Śubhaveśi*, *Pāpaveśi*, *Miśraveśi*).
- **Vośi Yoga:** Planet in 12th from Sun (*Śubhavośi*, *Pāpavośi*, *Miśravośi*).
- **Ubhayācarī Yoga:** Planets in both 2nd and 12th from Sun (*Śubha-Ubhayācarī*, *Pāpa-Ubhayācarī*, *Miśra-Ubhayācarī*).

---

## 7. Dual-Lagna Raja Yoga (Ubhayalagna)
Formed when a Kendra-Koṇa Raja Yoga combination operates simultaneously from Janma Lagna and Candra Lagna, harmonizing external action with internal emotional fulfillment.
