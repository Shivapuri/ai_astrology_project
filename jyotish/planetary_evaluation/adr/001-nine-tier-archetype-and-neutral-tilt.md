# ADR-001: 9-Tier Behavioral Archetype Spectrum & Neutral Sign Dynamic Tilt

## Context & The Shortcoming in the Naive Model
In the classical 4-Quadrant diagnostic matrix (Vic DiCara), planets are sorted by two primary axes:
1. **Moral Character / Intent (Dignity):** High vs. Low.
2. **Kinetic Horsepower / Stamina (Shadbala):** High vs. Low.

Under this binary split, any planet with below 50% dignity (neutral, enemy, or debilitated signs) and high Shadbala was automatically classified into the alarming archetype **`⚔️ The Armed Dictator`** (high muscle armed with rash or destructive intent).

### The Real-Life Case: Shivapuri Baba
* **Chart:** Shivapuri Baba (Self-realized master and yogic ascetic).
* **Placement:** Mars in Virgo at 15°, with 120% Shadbala (*Total Virūpas* 360+), conjoined by Moon and Jupiter aspects, in *Mudita Avasthā* (delighted state).
* **The Failure:** Because Virgo is technically a "Neutral" sign (*Sama Kshetra*, baseline 37.5% dignity), the naive binary model classified this peaceful, enlightened sage's Mars as **The Armed Dictator**! This was a severe diagnostic failure.

## Decision
1. **Expand from 4 Quadrants to a 9-Tier Matrix:**
   Split both axes into three calibrated bands:
   - **Quality (Dignity):** High ($\ge 55\%$), Neutral ($35\% - 55\%$), Low ($< 35\%$).
   - **Muscle (Shadbala):** High ($\ge 110\%$), Balanced ($88\% - 110\%$), Low ($< 88\%$).
2. **Introduce the Neutral Sign Dynamic Tilt (*Sama Kshetra*):**
   Neutral signs are not rigid; they act like pragmatic business partners who adapt to their surroundings. When a neutral sign receives benefic aspects (*Śubha Dṛṣṭi*), joyful feeling states (*Mudita* or *Garvita*), or strong host support, its effective dignity dynamically tilts upward from 37.5% up to **54.9%**.
3. **Establish `⚒️ The Pragmatic Executive` Archetype:**
   Planets with neutral dignity and high kinetic muscle are diagnosed as **The Pragmatic Executive** (a tireless champion, practical craftsman, and unpretentious powerhouse).

## Proof & Validation
Shivapuri Baba's Mars in Virgo now receives an effective dignity tilt to 54.9% under his Moon/Jupiter rays and Mudita state. Equipped with 120% Shadbala, he is diagnosed as **`⚒️ The Pragmatic Executive`** with a solid vitality score of **★ 7.5 / 10 Capable**, perfectly capturing his lifelong disciplined endurance without false tyrannical stigma.
