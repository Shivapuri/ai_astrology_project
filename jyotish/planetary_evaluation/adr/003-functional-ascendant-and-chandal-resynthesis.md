# ADR-003: Ascendant Functional Roles & Affliction Resynthesis (The Himmler Paradigm)

## Context & The Shortcoming in the Naive Model
Standard planetary evaluation systems rely on two isolated pillars: Essential Dignity (sign placement) and Shadbala (horsepower). 

Under this naive approach, a severe paradox emerges whenever a planet has peak essential dignity and enormous kinetic muscle, but is conjoined with dark nodal/malefic forces and programmed by the rising sign to act as a ruthless material driver.

### The Real-Life Case: Heinrich Himmler
* **Chart:** Heinrich Himmler (Architect of the Nazi SS and the Holocaust).
* **Placement:** Jupiter in Sagittarius at 7°46' (*Moolatrikona* / peak royal office, 87.5% dignity), with 112% Shadbala. Conjoined Rahu at 4.87° separation and Saturn at 21.64°. Rising sign is Aquarius (*Kumbha*).
* **The Dual Failures of Prior Models:**
  1. *The Naive Benefic Fallacy:* Because Jupiter is in Moolatrikona with high Shadbala, the standard two-pillar model stamped Himmler's Jupiter as **`🌟 The Generous King`** (a noble, selfless benefactor conferring expansive blessings)! This is historically and ethically absurd.
  2. *The Arbitrary Demolition Fallacy:* If an engine arbitrarily slashes the score to 1.0 because Himmler was evil, it fails to explain how he had the immense administrative leverage, stamina, and organizational capability to build and mobilize a massive empire-wide apparatus.

## Decision
1. **Integrate BPHS Chapter 34 Functional Roles:**
   A planet's natural nature is filtered through its rising sign:
   - For Aquarius Lagna, Jupiter rules the **11th House (*Trishadāya*)** of aggressive worldly ambition and vast social networks, and the **2nd House (*Māraka*)** of resources. This transforms Jupiter into a potent **Functional Malefic**.
2. **Detect Classical Nodal Afflictions (`jyotish/yogas/chandal_yogas.py`):**
   - Conjunction with Rahu within 5° triggers **Guru-Chāṇḍāla Yoga** (ideological zeal, subversion of dharma, taboo dogma).
3. **Detect Deeptādi Vikala Avasthā:**
   - Conjunction with two or more cruel planets (Rahu + Saturn) triggers **Vikala Avasthā** (besieged / mutilated operational climate).
4. **Preserve Kinetic Horsepower while Re-synthesizing the Archetype:**
   - Keep the real-world executive scale intact (**★ 7.9 / 10 Capable**).
   - Re-synthesize the archetypal title from "The Generous King" to **`⚡ Ideological Mobilizer`** (or *Embattled Executive*), accompanied by explicit red warning badges.

## Proof & Validation
Himmler's Jupiter is accurately diagnosed as **`The Generous King (⚡ Ideological Mobilizer)`** with **★ 7.9 / 10 Capable**. The diagnosis honestly reports his terrifying real-world organizing power while exposing the fanatical, taboo ideological distortion that drove it.
