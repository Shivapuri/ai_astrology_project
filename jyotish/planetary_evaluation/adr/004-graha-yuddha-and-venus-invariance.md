# ADR-004: Graha Yuddha (Planetary War) & The Venus Invariance Rule

## Context & The Shortcoming in the Naive Model
When two physical planets are in an extremely tight conjunction, classical Vedic astrology treats this as *Graha Yuddha* (Planetary War). The defeated planet suffers *Nipidita Avasthā* (oppressed/conquered state), losing its capacity to freely manifest its significations.

Many modern astrological tools either ignore planetary wars completely (treating them as normal conjunctions) or miscalculate the winner using zodiacal longitude instead of celestial latitude.

### The Real-Life Case: Adolf Hitler
* **Chart:** Adolf Hitler.
* **Placement:** Mars at 24°10' Taurus and Venus at 24°35' Taurus (within 0°25' in the same sign).
* **The Failure:** If treated as a generic conjunction, Mars and Venus are simply blended together. If evaluated by longitude, Venus appears ahead in degree, but longitude does not determine victory in classical texts.

## Decision
1. **Classical Eligibility & Orb Threshold (*Phaladeepika* 4.2 & *BPHS*):**
   - Planetary War occurs **strictly between the 5 starry planets (*Tara Grahas*)**: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn.
   - The Sun combusts (*Asta*), the Moon occults, and Rahu/Ketu shadow; they do not engage in war.
   - The war orb threshold is $\le 1^\circ 00'$ (60 arcminutes) within the **same sign**.
2. **The Venus Invariance Rule (*Bahula-Ruchi*):**
   - Venus **NEVER loses a planetary war**. Classical texts (*Brihat Samhita* and *Phaladeepika*) state that due to its supreme natural luminosity, brilliance, and grace, Venus remains invincible in war.
3. **Northern Celestial Latitude Rule (*Udaka*):**
   - For all other planetary pairs, the planet with the higher (more northern) celestial latitude wins the war.
   - Fallback: Higher Shadbala Virūpas.
4. **Consequences for Victor & Defeated:**
   - **Defeated Planet (*Nipidita*):** Receives the **`⚔️ Nipidita (War Defeat)`** badge, reflecting crushed autonomy.
   - **Victor Planet (*Jayi*):** Receives the **`🏆 War Victor`** badge, bearing a **Combat Stain** reflecting the heavy energy of the battle.

## Proof & Validation
In Hitler's chart, Venus defeats Mars via the Venus Invariance Rule. Mars is stamped with **`⚔️ Nipidita (War Defeat via Venus)`**. Conjoined with the Sun and combust, Hitler's Mars is diagnosed as **`⚔️ The Armed Dictator`** with a sunken vitality score of **★ 3.2 / 10 Severe Hazard**, explaining his frantic, self-destructive military impulses.
