# Sign Attributes & Planetary Distribution Specifications

## 1. Context & Scriptural Foundation
In classical Jyotish (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra Ch. 4, Jataka Tattva Ch. 1, Saravali Ch. 34), each of the twelve signs (Rāśis) possesses foundational classifications that dictate how the planets situated within them manifest their energies.

Following the core principle from *The Core Triad of Sign Attributes*:
> "To understand the characteristics of each sign it helps to remember (1) its polarity, (2) its quality, and (3) its element. Avoid embellishing the sign with other characteristics."

## 2. Core Triad Specifications

### A. Polarity & Gender (Active vs. Passive)
- **Odd Signs (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11)**: Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius.
  - Nature: Active, masculine, day signs. Extroverted, expressive, and direct.
  - Planets favored: Masculine planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter).
- **Even Signs (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12)**: Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces.
  - Nature: Passive, feminine, night signs. Introverted, reflective, and receptive.
  - Planets favored: Feminine planets (Moon, Venus).

### B. Quality & Mobility (Gunas / Charadi)
- **Cardinal / Movable (Chara)**: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn.
  - Nature: Outgoing, enterprising, initiatory, indicates sudden movement or change.
- **Fixed (Sthira)**: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius.
  - Nature: Steadfast, resistant to sudden change, preserving the status quo.
- **Mutable / Dual (Dvisvabhava)**: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces.
  - Nature: Adaptable, flexible junction points. (The first 15° acts like fixed, the last 15° acts like movable).

### C. The Four Elements (Tattvas)
- **Fire (Agni)**: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius.
  - Traits: Assertive, open, courageous, aspirational, visionary.
- **Earth (Prithvi)**: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn.
  - Traits: Practical, grounded, sensory, steady.
- **Air (Vayu)**: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius.
  - Traits: Sociable, communicative, rational, mentally curious.
- **Water (Jala)**: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces.
  - Traits: Sensitive, instinctive, emotional, intuitive.

## 3. Social Archetypes (Varnas) & Ayurvedic Humors (Doshas)
- **Varnas:**
  - Fire Signs: **Kshatriya** (warriors, leaders, executives — upholding law, protection).
  - Earth Signs: **Shudra** (craftsmen, service workers, builders — stability, practical labor).
  - Air Signs: **Vaishya** (merchants, communicators — commerce, trade, rational inquiry).
  - Water Signs: **Brahmin** (scholars, counselors, priests — wisdom, counseling, spirituality).
- **Doshas:**
  - Fire signs feed **Pitta** (metabolic fire, digestion, heat).
  - Air signs feed **Vata** (nervous energy, movement, dryness).
  - Earth & Water signs feed **Kapha** (physical mass, bodily lubrication, endurance).

## 4. Rising Orientation (Udaya)
- **Head-rising (Shirshodaya)**: Gemini, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius.
  - Result: Favorable/auspicious; yields fruits in early life or at the start of a planetary cycle (Dasha).
- **Back-rising (Prishtodaya)**: Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn.
  - Result: Yields results in later stages of life or towards the end of a planetary cycle.
- **Both-ways rising (Ubhayodaya)**: Pisces.
  - Result: Fruitful and active at all times throughout life.

## 5. Kalapurusha Body Regions (Head-to-Toe Anatomy)
The 12 signs represent the physical limbs and bodily functions of the cosmic personification of time (*Kalapurusha*):
1. **Aries**: Head, brain, scalp, eyes
2. **Taurus**: Face, neck, throat, larynx, vocal cords
3. **Gemini**: Shoulders, arms, hands, upper chest/lungs
4. **Cancer**: Chest, breasts, stomach, ribs
5. **Leo**: Heart, solar plexus, upper back
6. **Virgo**: Navel, small intestines, colon, digestive tract
7. **Libra**: Lower abdomen, lumbar region, kidneys
8. **Scorpio**: Excretory and reproductive organs, pelvic base
9. **Sagittarius**: Hips, thighs, arterial system
10. **Capricorn**: Knees, kneecaps, skeletal joints
11. **Aquarius**: Calves, shins, ankles, skin
12. **Pisces**: Feet, toes, lymphatic fluid system
