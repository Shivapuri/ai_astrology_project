## 📌 2-Minute Executive Summary

Mercury (*Budha*) is the planetary indicator of analytical intellect (*Buddhi*), communication, discrimination, commercial trade, humor, and cognitive agility. Its placement by sign reveals **how you process information, how you speak and write, your learning style, and how you solve practical problems**.

This masterclass note provides the overarching astrological rules, dignities, and elemental dynamics of Mercury across all 12 zodiac signs, linking directly to each of the 12 detailed sign-by-sign study guides in the vault.

---

## 🔑 Key Astrological Rules for Mercury in Signs

1. **The Principle of Discrimination & Communication:**
   - Mercury's role is to categorize, name, calculate, and connect. In earth and air signs, it excels in factual logic, coding, commerce, and communication; in water and fire signs, it becomes intuitive, subjective, creative, or ideological.
2. **Dignity Spectrum (*Avastha*):**
   - **Exalted (*Param Ucha* at 15°) & Moolatrikona (15°-20°):** **Virgo** (*Kanya*) — Peak analytical precision, editing, diagnostic medicine, and operational logic.
   - **Own Sign (*Swakshetra*):** **Gemini** (*Mithuna*) — Versatile multitasking, wit, linguistic speed, and intellectual curiosity.
   - **Debilitated (*Param Neecha* at 15°):** **Pisces** (*Meena*) — Linear logic dissolved in water; functions as an 'assistive intellect' channeling poetic imagination, symbolism, and spiritual intuition.
   - **Detriment Signs:** **Sagittarius** (grand philosophy overriding fine print and details).
