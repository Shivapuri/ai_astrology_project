## 📌 2-Minute Executive Summary

Jupiter (*Guru* / *Brihaspati*) is the great cosmic benefic, representing wisdom, morality (*Dharma*), expansion, fortune, optimism, children, and mentorship. Wherever Jupiter sits by sign reveals **how you cultivate faith, what moral principles guide your life, and how you expand your consciousness**.

This masterclass note provides the overarching astrological rules, dignities, and elemental dynamics of Jupiter across all 12 zodiac signs, linking directly to each of the 12 detailed sign-by-sign study guides in the vault.

---

## 🔑 Key Astrological Rules for Jupiter in Signs

1. **The Principle of Expansion & Dharma:**
   - Jupiter brings growth and optimism. In harmonious signs, it elevates character and bestows genuine wisdom; in conflicting signs, it can manifest as excessive indulgence, ideological dogmatism, or cynicism.
2. **Dignity Spectrum (*Avastha*):**
   - **Exalted (*Param Ucha* at 5°):** **Cancer** (*Karka*) — Jupiter reaches its highest joy in the Moon's cardinal water sign, blending higher wisdom with unconditional maternal empathy and emotional generosity.
   - **Moolatrikona (0°-10°) & Own Sign (10°-30°):** **Sagittarius** (*Dhanus*) — Pure philosophical vision, ethical scholarship, and cosmic justice.
   - **Own Sign (*Swakshetra*):** **Pisces** (*Meena*) — Oceanic compassion, imaginative artistry, and spiritual surrender (*Moksha*).
   - **Debilitated (*Param Neecha* at 5°):** **Capricorn** (*Makara*) — In Saturn's cold, pragmatic earth sign, Jupiter's faith is challenged by skepticism, materialism, and commercial bureaucracy.
3. **The Benefic Multiplier:** Because Jupiter is naturally gracious, even in difficult signs it protects the houses it aspects (5th, 7th, and 9th aspects).
