# Lesson 03: 9 Very Important Fundamental Yogas for the Sun and Ascendant

## 📌 2-Minute Executive Summary
Covers essential solar and ascendant combinations including Vesi, Vasi, Ubhayachari (planets flanking the Sun), and Kartari yogas (planets flanking the Ascendant). The Sun represents purpose, confidence, and soul-force, while the Ascendant represents the physical vessel and social persona. Mantreswara teaches how benefic and malefic planets flanking these pivotal points either protect or hem in the native's vitality.

---

## 🔑 Key Astrological Takeaways
- **Vesi Yoga (Planets in 2nd from Sun):** Bestows articulate speech, steady memory, and financial stability.
- **Vasi Yoga (Planets in 12th from Sun):** Imparts spiritual inclinations, charitable nature, and good reputation.
- **Ubhayachari Yoga (Planets flanking both 2nd & 12th from Sun):** Creates a harmonious, balanced individual endowed with charm and royal favor.
- **Shubha Kartari (Benefics in 2nd and 12th from Lagna):** Creates a protective shield around the body and personality, ensuring health and prosperity.
- **Papa Kartari (Malefics in 2nd and 12th from Lagna):** Hems in the native, producing physical vulnerability, anxiety, and uphill battles.

## 📝 High-Fidelity Condensed Lecture

### 1. Introduction to Neighboring Yogas

This lecture continues our study of *Phaladeepika*, Chapter 6, focusing on yogas (planetary combinations) formed by planets neighboring the Sun and Ascendant. Previously, we examined:

*   **Planets in Dignity and Strength:** How Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn create special yogas when both dignified (e.g., in own or exalted sign) and strong (e.g., in an angle to the Ascendant or Moon). Such planets impart many of their qualities to the native.
*   **Moon-Neighboring Yogas:** Yogas formed when planets occupy the zodiac sign immediately before (12th house) or after (2nd house) the Moon, or both. These were termed "follower" (2nd), "leader" (12th), "surrounding" (both), and "lonesome" (none).

This week, we apply a similar formula to the Sun and Ascendant. The Moon is most sensitive to these neighboring influences, the Sun less so, and the Ascendant least, but all three can form significant yogas.

### 2. Sun-Neighboring Yogas

The *Phaladeepika* text states that planets (excluding the Moon) in the 2nd or 12th house from the Sun form yogas. Unlike the Moon yogas, Sun yogas differentiate between benefic and malefic planets.

#### 2.1 Definitions of Sun Yogas

*   **Planet in the 2nd House from the Sun:** Called a **Supporter** (*Vaishi*).
    *   If benefic: **Auspicious Supporter** (*Shubha Vaishi*).
    *   If malefic: **Inauspicious Supporter** (*Papa Vaishi*).
*   **Planet in the 12th House from the Sun:** Called a **Neighbor** (*Vasi*).
    *   If benefic: **Auspicious Neighbor** (*Shubha Vasi*).
    *   If malefic: **Inauspicious Neighbor** (*Papa Vasi*).
*   **Planets in Both 2nd and 12th Houses from the Sun:** Called **Surrounders** (*Ubhayachari*).
    *   If both are benefic: **Auspicious Surrounders**.
    *   If both are malefic: **Inauspicious Surrounders**.
    *   *Note:* This yoga only forms if both planets are of the same type (both benefic or both malefic). If one is benefic and the other malefic, it does not form an *Ubhayachari* yoga.

#### 2.2 Clarifying Benefic/Malefic and Auspicious/Inauspicious

It's crucial to understand the astrological meaning beyond literal English translations:

*   **Benefic (*Saumya*):** Soft, gentle, forgiving, intelligent, feminine. Not necessarily "good in a fight."
*   **Malefic (*Kroora*):** Tough, hard, strong, powerful, masculine, not very forgiving or gentle. "Good in a fight," but not necessarily "good in a family."
*   **Auspicious (*Shubha*):** Nice, pleasant, favorable.
*   **Inauspicious (*Papa*):** Difficult, not nice, rough, tough, hard.

#### 2.3 Results of Auspicious Sun Yogas

These yogas indicate that the Sun receives support or influence according to the benefic nature of the neighboring planets.

*   **Auspicious Supporter (Benefic in 2nd from Sun):** The Sun receives internal support, leading to:
    *   Luck (*Bhagya*) and enjoyment (*Sukha*).
    *   Qualifications and patience.
    *   Commanding nature (*Nripa*), stability, and integrity.
*   **Auspicious Neighbor (Benefic in 12th from Sun):** The Sun's external expression is influenced, leading to:
    *   Being well-known yet modest.
    *   Endearing to everyone (*Sakalapriya*).
    *   Very lucky and generous.
    *   Dear to women (*Mahesha Priya*).
    *   *Reasoning:* The planet in the 12th house is considered to "lead" the Sun, influencing how the Sun extroverts.
*   **Auspicious Surrounders (Benefics in both 2nd and 12th from Sun):** Combines the best of both influences, resulting in:
    *   Great beauty (*Charanga*) and an endearing/accommodating voice (*Priyavak*).
    *   Tasteful/artistic nature (*Rasika*).
    *   Eloquence (*Vagmi*).
    *   Renown (*Yashasvi*) and wealth (*Dhany*).

#### 2.4 Results of Inauspicious Sun Yogas

These yogas indicate that the Sun receives support or influence according to the malefic nature of the neighboring planets, often hardening the Sun's qualities.

*   **Inauspicious Supporter (Malefic in 2nd from Sun):** The Sun receives malefic power, making it strict, hard, and self-focused, leading to:
    *   Undeserved insult (*Ninda*).
    *   Dampened splendor (*Hataruchi*).
    *   Friendlessness (*Hina Priyo*) and a base nature (*Urjana*).
*   **Inauspicious Neighbor (Malefic in 12th from Sun):** The Sun's external expression is influenced by malefic qualities, leading to:
    *   Deception (*Mayavi*) and insulting others (*Paranindaka*).
    *   Partnering with rude or callous individuals (*Kala*).
    *   A tendency to do difficult things and skill with weapons (*Astra Adhika*), indicating a fighter.
    *   *Reasoning:* The malefic in the 12th leads the Sun, causing it to extrovert with malefic traits.
*   **Inauspicious Surrounders (Malefics in both 2nd and 12th from Sun):** This is the most challenging combination, resulting in:
    *   Deserved insult from society (*Loka Siat Apakurti*).
    *   Sadness.
    *   Inability to benefit from wisdom (*Vidya*), wealth (*Bhaga*), or opportunity (*Aishvarya*).

#### 2.5 Nuances and Qualifications for Sun Yogas

*   **Nodes (Rahu and Ketu):** Are not considered planets for the formation of these specific yogas in classical texts.
*   **Retrogression:** While not explicitly mentioned for these yogas, retrogression generally intensifies a planet and can alter its indications.
*   **Conditional Benefics/Malefics:** While starting with natural benefics and malefics is standard, advanced practice may reveal that planets acting as conditional malefics (e.g., 6th or 3rd lords) can modify these yoga results.
*   **Purity of Influence (*Sushubha*):** The text introduces the concept of *Sushubha* (very auspicious). This refers to a benefic in the 2nd house from the Sun that is free from malefic aspects or conjunctions. This implies a spectrum: the more purely benefic the influence, the more auspicious the results. Conversely, a malefic without benefic influence would be purely inauspicious.

### 3. Ascendant-Neighboring Yogas

The Ascendant is the least receptive to neighboring influences, forming only one type of yoga.

#### 3.1 Definition of Ascendant Yoga

*   **Planets in Both 2nd and 12th Houses from the Ascendant:** Called **Scissors** (*Kartari*).
    *   If both are benefic: **Auspicious Scissors** (*Shubha Kartari*).
    *   If both are malefic: **Inauspicious Scissors** (*Papa Kartari*).
    *   *Note:* The "leading/following" concept does not apply to the Ascendant, as the Ascendant is the horizon point around which the sky moves.

#### 3.2 Results of Ascendant Yogas

These yogas primarily affect the native's physical body, health, and general well-being.

*   **Auspicious Scissors (Benefics in both 2nd and 12th from Ascendant):**
    *   Long life (*Jeeva*).
    *   Absence of worry (*Nirvighna*), careful nature (*Viroga*).
    *   No disease (*Viripu*) and no enemies.
    *   Beauty.
*   **Inauspicious Scissors (Malefics in both 2nd and 12th from Ascendant):**
    *   Short life.
    *   Prone to illness (*Asuchi* - implying unhygienic or susceptible to infection).
    *   Lack of family happiness.
    *   Lack of beauty.

### 4. General Principle of Neighboring Yogas

The text concludes by emphasizing a fundamental principle:

*   **Auspicious yogas for the Moon, Sun, and Ascendant indicate auspicious results.**
*   **Inauspicious yogas indicate the opposite.**

This means that when benefic planets form these yogas, they *beneficialize* the focal point (Moon, Sun, or Ascendant), imparting their soft, gentle, and favorable qualities. Conversely, when malefic planets form these yogas, they *maleficize* the focal point, imparting their tough, hard, and challenging qualities. The purity and strength of these benefic or malefic influences directly correlate with the intensity of the resulting effects.
