# Simple Guide: How Planetary Friendships Work

In Jyotish, planets are like people. They have their own inborn personalities, but they also react to who is sitting next to them in a room. To figure out how two planets feel about each other, the software does a 3-step calculation:

## 1. Natural Friendship (Naisargika)
**The Concept:** This is the permanent, inborn "chemistry" between two planets. Just like some people naturally get along, Jupiter and the Sun are natural friends. 
**The Jyotish Rule:** To calculate this, the software looks at a planet's favorite home sign, called its **Moolatrikona**. For example, the Sun's Moolatrikona is Leo. The software then counts specific houses away from Leo (2nd, 12th, 5th, 9th, 4th, 8th). The planets that rule those houses are the Sun's "Natural Friends." Everyone else is an Enemy (or Neutral if they rule one friend house and one enemy house).

## 2. Temporary Friendship (Tatkalika)
**The Concept:** This is based purely on physical distance in the sky on the exact day you were born. Even if two planets are natural enemies, if they give each other enough "personal space," they agree to a temporary truce. If they are crammed into the same room, they annoy each other and become temporary enemies.
**The Jyotish Rule:** The software looks at the main birth chart (D1/Rasi). If a planet is sitting 3 signs in front or 3 signs behind another planet (Houses 2, 3, 4, 10, 11, 12), they are Temporary Friends. If they are conjunct in the same house (House 1), or sitting awkwardly in Houses 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, they are Temporary Enemies.

## 3. Compound Friendship (Panchadha)
**The Concept:** This is the final, ultimate score. The software simply adds the Natural score and the Temporary score together.
**The Jyotish Rule:** 
* Friend + Friend = **Great Friend** (Adhimitra)
* Friend + Neutral = **Friend** (Mitra)
* Enemy + Friend = **Neutral** (Sama)
* Neutral + Enemy = **Enemy** (Shatru)
* Enemy + Enemy = **Great Enemy** (Adhishatru)

Whenever the software says a planet is in a "Great Friend's Sign," it is using this final, 5-level Compound Friendship score.
