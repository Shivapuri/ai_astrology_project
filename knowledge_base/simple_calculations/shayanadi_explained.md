# Simple Guide: Understanding Shayanadi Avasthas (The 12 Activity States)

## What is a Shayanadi Avastha?
In Vedic astrology, **Avastha** means the "state" or "condition" of a planet. 

While other calculations tell us a planet's age (Bala Avastha) or its mood (Deeptadi Avastha), the **Shayanadi Avasthas** describe what the planet is actively **doing** at the moment of birth. Think of it like catching someone in the middle of their day: are they working in a meeting, relaxing on the couch, eating dinner, dancing, or sleeping?

There are **12 Activity States** and **3 Sub-States** that reveal how strongly the planet performs its activity.

---

## The 12 Activity States (What the Planet is Doing)

1. **Shayana (Lying Down / Resting):** The planet is resting in bed. It is awake, but lacks the drive or physical initiative to get up and execute tasks.
2. **Upaveshana (Sitting):** The planet is seated comfortably. It is in a receptive, waiting state—ready to listen and observe.
3. **Netrapani (Hand on Eye):** The planet has its hand over its eyes. It is looking carefully, being cautious, or feeling stressed by what it sees.
4. **Prakashana (Shining / Illuminating):** The planet is glowing brightly in the spotlight. It expresses its best qualities, sharing wisdom and good deeds.
5. **Gamana (Departing / On the Move):** The planet is packing its bags and leaving. It creates movement, travel, and restlessness.
6. **Agamana (Arriving / Coming Home):** The planet is returning home from a journey, bringing back accomplishments and rewards.
7. **Sabhavasati (In Assembly / Council):** The planet is sitting in a royal court or board meeting. It has great leadership, diplomatic skill, and social influence.
8. **Agama (Acquiring / Gathering):** The planet is actively collecting resources, wealth, or knowledge.
9. **Bhojana (Eating / Feasting):** The planet is sitting down at the dinner table enjoying a meal. It enjoys comfort and nourishment.
10. **Nrityalipsa (Longing to Dance):** The planet is full of rhythm and artistic enthusiasm, wanting to celebrate, perform, or play music.
11. **Kautuka (Eager / Curious):** The planet is in a playful, joyous mood—exploring hobbies, having fun, and learning new things.
12. **Nidra (Slumber / Deep Sleep):** The planet is fast asleep. Its energy is dormant and hidden beneath the surface.

## How the Calculation Works (In Simple Terms)

To find out which of the 12 activities a planet is doing, the computer combines five timing pieces:

1. **Planet's Star Number (Nakshatra):** Which of the 27 stellar constellations (nakshatras) the planet occupies (1 to 27).
2. **Planet's Identity Number:** A fixed traditional number for each celestial body (Sun=1, Moon=2, Mars=3, Mercury=4, Jupiter=5, Venus=6, Saturn=7, Rahu=8, Ketu=9).
3. **Quarter of the Star (Pada):** Each 27-star constellation is divided into 4 quarters called **padas** (numbered 1, 2, 3, or 4). Ernst Wilhelm's method strictly uses this 1–4 quarter number.
4. **Ascendant Sign (Lagna):** The rising zodiac sign at birth, numbered from 1 (Aries) to 12 (Pisces).
5. **Moon's Star (Janma Nakshatra):** The star constellation the Moon occupied at birth (1 to 27).
6. **Time Elapsed from Sunrise (Ishta Ghati):** Time measured in traditional ancient 24-minute blocks (called **ghatis**) counted from that morning's local sunrise.

### The Math:
1. Multiply: `(Planet Star × Planet Number × Star Quarter)`
2. Add: `+ Ascendant Sign + Moon Star + Ghatis from Sunrise`
3. Divide the grand total by **12**. The remainder (1 to 12) points directly to the planet's activity!

## The 3 Sub-States (How Strongly the Activity Manifests)

Once we know the planet's activity, a second calculation checks how much real-world power that activity has. It uses the sound of the person's first name (**Varnamashka**) and a special planet number:

* **Cheshta (Active / Strong — 100% Power):** The planet is fully in motion. Its activity creates visible, powerful life events.
* **Drishti (Apparent / Medium — 50% Power):** The planet is watching from nearby. Its activity produces moderate, noticeable results.
* **Vicheshta (Motionless / Inactive — 10% Power):** The planet is frozen or passive. Its activity operates quietly in the background.
