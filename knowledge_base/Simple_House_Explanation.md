# Simple Explanation: Ascendant, Bhavas, and Houses

This document provides a simple, jargon-free explanation of how the core framework of the chart is built based on Ernst Wilhelm's current *Kala* methodology.

## 1. The Ascendant is just a point in space
Imagine you are standing outside at the exact moment and location you were born, and you look directly East towards the horizon. The **Ascendant** is simply the exact degree of the Zodiac (the stars) that is rising over the horizon at that exact second. 

It is pure astronomy. It is just an actual point sitting in a specific sign (for example, at $9^\circ 35'$ in the sign of Leo).

## 2. How the Bhavas (Whole Sign Houses) are made
For the primary reading of the chart, we use the **Whole Sign** system. The golden rule is: **One Zodiac Sign = One Entire House**.

Because the Ascendant is simply a point sitting inside a sign, it does not define the middle or the edge of the house. It just tells us *which* sign is House 1.

**Here is exactly how it works:**
1. We see that the Ascendant is sitting at $9^\circ 35'$ inside **Leo**.
2. To find the beginning of the 1st House, we simply go $9^\circ 35'$ **backward** to hit the exact start of the Leo sign ($0^\circ$).
3. To find the end of the 1st House, we go the rest of the way **forward** to hit the exact end of the Leo sign ($30^\circ$).
4. This makes the **entire sign of Leo** your 1st House. The Ascendant is just a VIP guest sitting inside the room, and the room itself is simply the entire sign.
5. From there, it's just counting in order. The entire next sign (Virgo, from $0^\circ$ to $30^\circ$) becomes your 2nd House, Libra becomes your 3rd House, and so on.

*In your circular chart, these Whole Sign houses are represented by the green dashed lines and the blue numbers in the center.*

## 3. The Campanus Cusps (A Completely Separate Layer)
Sometimes astrologers want to ignore the rigid 30-degree Zodiac signs and slice the physical sky up like an orange based on complex 3D geometry. This is called the **Campanus** system.

This is a completely separate layer of math from the Whole Sign houses.
* In the Campanus system, the math draws a fresh line right at your Ascendant degree ($9^\circ 35'$ Leo) and uses it as the strict **starting edge (cusp)** of a 3D slice. 
* The other 11 slices (cusps 2, 3, 4, etc.) are drawn based on physical spatial geometry. Because of this 3D slicing, these pieces will almost never line up perfectly with the 30-degree Zodiac signs.

*In your circular chart, these complex Campanus cusps are represented by the small floating numbers on the outer edge (red for the major angles like 1, 4, 7, 10, and violet for the rest). They act as highly sensitive points floating inside your main Whole Sign houses.*
