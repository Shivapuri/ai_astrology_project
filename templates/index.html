What are Nakshatras?
Nakshatras are the 27 lunar mansions of Vedic astrology. While the 12 zodiac signs show the broad environment, the Nakshatras reveal the deeper psychological focus and specific energy of a planet. Each Nakshatra is divided into 4 Padas (steps or quarters), which fine-tune how the planet behaves.

Planetary Dignity

What is Planetary Dignity?
Dignity measures how welcomed and comfortable a planet feels in its current sign. A planet in its own sign or exalted is like a king in his castle (strong, positive, and capable). A planet in an enemy's sign or debilitated is like a guest in a hostile house (stressed and struggling to do its job). This uses the 5-fold compound friendship rules (Panchadha Maitri).

Core Avasthas (States)

What are Planetary States (Avasthas)?
Avasthas are the "states of being" or moods of a planet. They modify how effectively the planet can do its job:
• Bala (Age): Represents the age and physical vitality of the planet, determining its sheer capacity and stamina to produce results.
• Jagrat (Alertness): Indicates the awakening of the planet's consciousness, showing whether it is awake and eager to act, slumbering, or somewhere in between.
• Deeptadi & Lajjitadi (Moods): Reveals the internal mood, ease, or stress the planet experiences while it produces events, coloring how the event will ultimately feel to you.
Graha Bala (Age) Jagrat (Alertness) Deeptadi (Condition) Lajjitadi (Moods)

Shadbala (Six-fold Strength)

What is Shadbala?
Shadbala calculates the total raw "horsepower" or physical capacity of a planet to influence your life. It compiles strength from six different astronomical sources (position, direction, time, motion, nature, and aspects).
It is measured in Rupas (where 1 Rupa = 60 Virupas).
• High (e.g., Above 7 Rupas): Very strong, highly active, and capable of overpowering other planets.
• Average (e.g., 5 to 7 Rupas): Standard strength, capable of doing its necessary work.
• Low (e.g., Below 5 Rupas): Weak, might struggle to produce its intended results independently.

Vimshottari Dasha (Saura Year)

What are Dashas?
Dashas are the planetary time periods that trigger the events promised in your birth chart. The "Mahadasha" is the major period ruled by a specific planet. During a planet's Mahadasha, its themes, its house placements, and its overall strength in your chart become the central focus of your life for that entire duration.

Rasi Drishti (Sign Aspects)

What are Rasi Aspects?
In Vedic astrology, signs (Rasis) aspect other signs directly based on their modality. If a planet sits in a sign, it fully aspects all planets located in the aspected signs. This is a binary "all-or-nothing" aspect used heavily in Jaimini astrology and for structural Yogas.

Graha Drishti to Planets (+/- Scores)

What are the +/- Scores?
This measures the exact Subha/Asubha Phala (Auspicious/Inauspicious impact) delivered from one planet to another based on Graha Drishti.

+ Score: The exact amount of positive support and blessings the planet is giving.
- Score: The exact amount of stress, harm, or affliction the planet is causing.

Aspects to House Cusps?
Houses have exact mathematical center points (cusps). This shows exactly how much a planet is blessing (+) or afflicting (-) that specific area of life by casting an aspect directly onto the exact degree of the house center.
