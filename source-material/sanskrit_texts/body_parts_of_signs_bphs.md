# Body Parts of the Signs in Hora Shastra (कालाङ्गानि / Kālāṅgāni)

This document contains the primary Sanskrit texts, word-by-word anatomical mappings, and English explanations from the foundational Hora Shastras (**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**, Chapter 4, and **Brihat Jataka**, Chapter 1).

---

## 1. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS) — Chapter 4: *Rāśi Svarūpa Adhyāya*

### Sanskrit Verses (Chapter 4, Verses 1–5)

```text
अहोरात्रस्य पूर्वान्त्यलोपाद् होराऽवशिष्यते ।
तस्य विज्ञानमात्रेण जातकर्मफलं वदेत् ॥ १॥

यदव्यक्तात्मको विष्णुः कालरूपो जनार्दनः ।
तस्याङ्गानि निबोध त्वं क्रमान्मेषादिराशयः ॥ २॥

मेषो वृषश्च मिथुनः कर्कसिंहकुमारिकाः ।
तुलालिऽश्च धनुर्नक्रे कुम्भो मीनस्ततः परम् ॥ ३॥

शीर्षानने तथा बाहू हृत्क्रोडकटिबस्तयः ।
गुह्योरुयुगले जानुयुग्मे वै जङ्घके तथा ॥ ४॥

चरणौ द्वौ तथा मेषात् ज्ञेयाः शीर्षादयः क्रमात् ।
चरस्थिरद्विस्वभावाः क्रूराक्रूरौ नरस्त्रियौ ॥ ५॥
```

### English Translation & Meaning

1. **Origin of Hora (Verse 1):**
   - The word **Hora** (the science of celestial time and natal astrology) is derived by removing the first syllable (*A*) and the last syllable (*tra*) from **Ahorātra** (day and night / a complete 24-hour cycle of time). By understanding this science, the fruit of past actions (*Karma*) and birth destiny can be known.

2. **The Cosmic Person — Kāla Puruṣa (Verses 2–3):**
   - The Supreme Lord (Vishnu / Janardana), though unmanifest, manifests in the visible universe as **Kāla** (Time Personified, known as the *Kāla Puruṣa*). His limbs (*aṅgas*) are formed sequentially by the 12 signs of the zodiac starting from Aries (*Meṣa*).

3. **Body Part Allocation (Verses 4–5):**
   The limbs of the Cosmic Person assigned to the signs from Aries (*Meṣa*) to Pisces (*Mīna*) are:
   - **Meṣa (Aries):** *Śīrṣa* — Head / Crown
   - **Vṛṣa (Taurus):** *Ānana* / *Mukha* — Face (eyes, mouth, nose, throat)
   - **Mithuna (Gemini):** *Bāhū* — The two Arms and Shoulders / Upper Chest
   - **Karka (Cancer):** *Hṛd* — Heart / Chest / Lungs
   - **Simha (Leo):** *Kroḍa* / *Udara* — Stomach / Upper Abdomen / Solar Plexus
   - **Kanyā (Virgo):** *Kaṭi* — Waist / Hips / Intestines
   - **Tulā (Libra):** *Basti* — Lower Abdomen / Pelvis / Bladder region
   - **Vṛścika (Scorpio):** *Guhya* — Private parts / Genitalia / Excretory organs
   - **Dhanu (Sagittarius):** *Ūru-yugala* — The two Thighs
   - **Makara (Capricorn):** *Jānu-yugma* — The two Knees
   - **Kumbha (Aquarius):** *Jaṅghe* — The two Calves / Shins / Ankles
   - **Mīna (Pisces):** *Caraṇau dvau* — The two Feet

## 2. Varahamihira's Brihat Jataka — Chapter 1, Verse 4

### Sanskrit Verse

```text
कालाङ्गानि वराङ्गमाननम् उरो हृत् क्रोड वासो भृतो
बस्तिर्व्यञ्जनम् ऊरु जानु युगुले जङ्घे ततोऽङ्घ्रि द्वयम् ।
मेषाश्वि प्रथमा नवऋक्ष चरणाश्चक्र स्थिताराशयो
राशि क्षेत्र गृहऋक्ष भानि भवनं चैकार्थ सम्प्रत्ययाः ॥ ४॥
```

### Anatomical Terms:
- **Varāṅga** (The best/foremost limb) = Head (*Meṣa* / Aries)
- **Ānanam** = Face (*Vṛṣa* / Taurus)
- **Uras** = Upper Chest / Shoulders (*Mithuna* / Gemini)
- **Hṛd** = Heart (*Karka* / Cancer)
- **Kroḍa** = Stomach / Belly (*Simha* / Leo)
- **Vāsobhṛta** (The belt/waist area) = Waist / Hips (*Kanyā* / Virgo)
- **Basti** = Pelvis / Lower abdomen / Urinary system (*Tulā* / Libra)
- **Vyañjanam** = Genitalia / Secret parts (*Vṛścika* / Scorpio)
- **Ūru** = Thighs (*Dhanu* / Sagittarius)
- **Jānu-yugala** = Pair of knees (*Makara* / Capricorn)
- **Jaṅghe** = Pair of shins/calves (*Kumbha* / Aquarius)
- **Aṅghri-dvayam** = Pair of feet (*Mīna* / Pisces)
